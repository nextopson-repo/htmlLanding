Frequently Asked Questions

Is Nextdeal free to use?

Yes. Downloading the app and browsing properties is free. We may introduce premium features for agents/owners later, but basic usage will always stay free.

Can I contact the owner/agent directly?

Yes. Unlike many portals, Nextdeal allows direct contact between buyers, owners, and agents - no unnecessary barriers.

Do I need to pay through the app?

No. Nextdeal does not handle property payments or bookings. All financial transactions should be done directly between parties after verification. This keeps your money safe and transparent.

I am not very tech-savvy. Can I still use Nextdeal?

Absolutely! The app is designed with a simple, step-by-step interface so even first-time users can post or search without confusion.
